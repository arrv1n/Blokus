1. Starting the game: Each player will use their first turn to place a piece in their corner of
the board.

2. Placing pieces: Pieces must be placed so that they are touching the corner of a piece of the
same colour. Pieces of the same colour cannot touch along their sides. Pieces of differing colours
can freely touch each other's side.
Once pieces are placed, they cannot be moved.

3. Rotating and flipping: In this version of the game, pieces are able to be rotated and flipped
only 10 times per game.
To flip/rotate, press .

4. Ending the game: The game ends when all players are no longer able to place anymore of their 
pieces.

5. Scoring: Player's scores are determined by counting the number of squares in the unplaced pieces.
The player with the lowest number of squares wins.
